Contact FAQ

Before you reach out

Straight answers. If yours is missing, call or message us.

Do you do a hard inquiry on my report?

No. Our free review uses a soft view of your report, so your score is not affected.

Is my data safe?

Documents are encrypted and shared only with the bureaus and lenders involved in your case, with your consent.

Can I speak in Hindi or Kannada?

Yes. Advisors speak English, Hindi, Kannada and Telugu.

Is the first call free?

Yes. The review and first call cost nothing.

Do you work on weekends?

We are closed on Sundays. Messages sent then are answered on Monday.
