FAQ

Questions about our services

Straight answers. If yours is missing, call or message us.

How long does credit repair take?

Simple errors are often resolved in 30 to 45 days. Settlements and older dues usually need three to six months.

Will you guarantee my score?

No. Nobody can promise a score. We promise a clear audit, honest timelines and every dispute filed on time.

Do you do a hard inquiry on my report?

No. Our free review uses a soft view of your report, so your score is not affected.

Can I repair my credit myself?

Yes. You can file disputes on your own. We save you the paperwork and follow-ups, and we know which fixes matter most.

Is my data safe?

Documents are encrypted and shared only with the bureaus and lenders involved in your case, with your consent.
